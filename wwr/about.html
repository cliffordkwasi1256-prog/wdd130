About Us - White Water Rafting

Our purpose is to connect people with the raw power and beauty of wild rivers — turning first-time paddlers into lifelong river stewards. Our mission is to deliver safe, adrenaline-pumping whitewater adventures for families, friends, and thrill-seekers, led by certified guides who put safety, fun, and the river first. Safety is the current that carries everything we do.

History

River Rush Rafting Co. began in the summer of 2010 with two guides, four secondhand rafts, and one unshakable belief: that everyone deserves to feel the thrill of the river.

What started as weekend trips down a local stretch of the river quickly grew into the region's most trusted outfitter. Fifteen years and thousands of adventurers later, we're still family-owned, still river-obsessed, and still running every trip like it's our first.
